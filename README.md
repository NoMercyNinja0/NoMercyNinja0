#### 🏋️ [GymDay](https://github.com/NoMercyNinja0/GymDay): workout and food log

[![Tests](https://github.com/NoMercyNinja0/GymDay/actions/workflows/tests.yml/badge.svg)](https://github.com/NoMercyNinja0/GymDay/actions/workflows/tests.yml)

An app for Android and iPhone, made for training in India.

- Build your own gym days from 220+ exercises and log sets in a few taps
- Track food from thousands of Indian and international dishes, or snap a photo of your meal
- See which muscles you've worked on a body map, and train with friends in clubs
- One plain JavaScript app inside native shells (Java on Android, Swift on iPhone), with a Supabase server and the Claude API for reading photos
- From the first build to App Store review in eleven days: [see every version](https://github.com/NoMercyNinja0/GymDay/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md)
